Acid

A 303-style monophonic acid bass synthesizer.

This installs the VST3 and Audio Unit versions of the plugin for all users:

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Acid.vst3
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Acid.component

Choose Customize to install only one of them. After installing, rescan plugins in your DAW (in Logic Pro, the Audio Unit is checked automatically).
